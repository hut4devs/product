# Hut4Devs Learning Resources

This directory contains learning pathways, courses, certifications, books, and technical references that help Hut4Devs collaborators build the skills needed to develop and maintain the product.

## Purpose

Our learning resources support the Hut4Devs Product Development Life Cycle:

**Discover  Define  Design  Build  Assure  Operate  Grow**

Resources are selected to help collaborators strengthen their specializations, apply their knowledge to Hut4Devs, and produce verifiable evidence of learning.

## Resource Sources

Our recommended learning resources draw from:

- **LinkedIn Learning**  professional courses and course-completion certificates.
- **Free learning platforms**  accessible courses, learning paths, badges, and certificates where offered.
- **Official technical documentation**  authoritative references for the technologies and standards used by Hut4Devs.
- **Books and e-books**  recommended reading for product management, design thinking, software engineering, security, testing, and other relevant disciplines.

### Z-Library and Book Resources

The project maintainer supports Z-Library through donations, and many of the recommended books may be sourced or discovered through that platform.

However, availability on a platform does not itself establish permission to redistribute a book. Collaborators should use legally available copies, publisher-authorized editions, open-access publications, or library access where applicable.

Hut4Devs does not intend this repository to host or redistribute copyrighted books without authorization. Where possible, we link to official, open-access, or otherwise authorized editions.

## Main Learning Roadmap

Start with:

- [Learning and Certification Roadmap](LEARNING_AND_CERTIFICATION_ROADMAP.md)

The roadmap prioritizes the product's current **Product Definition & Discovery** phase before outlining learning pathways for later lifecycle phases and the project's different specializations.

## How Collaborators Should Use These Resources

1. Choose resources relevant to your specialization and current product phase.
2. Prioritize material that addresses an actual Hut4Devs need.
3. Apply what you learn through documentation, research, designs, code, tests, or other project contributions.
4. Record completed courses and certifications accurately; distinguish professional certifications from course-completion certificates and badges.
5. Share useful findings and improvements with the team.

## Keeping This Directory Useful

When proposing a new resource, include its title, provider or author, link, cost, date or version where available, relevance to Hut4Devs, and the practical outcome collaborators should gain.

Prefer current, authoritative, accessible resources. Review links and availability periodically.
